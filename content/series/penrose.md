Latitude 5580 chassis, mainboard swapped for the Precision 3520 Xeon one (E3-1505M v6, ECC, TB3), stock BIOS replaced with coreboot + edk2 via an SPI clip. Writeups are the firmware work; worklogs are the parts, the benchmarks and the fan curve.
